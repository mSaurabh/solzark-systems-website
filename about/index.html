About

Enterprise experience, without the layers

Solzark Systems is a Houston-based IT consulting firm founded by Saurabh Mankar. The person scoping your engagement is the person building it.

Fifteen years of delivery sits behind the firm: analytical data stores for regulated utilities, AI-powered search infrastructure for a healthcare SaaS platform, multi-million-dollar programs, and engineering teams distributed across continents. The work spans the full lifecycle, from roadmap and requirements through build, QA and stakeholder handoff.

Consulting firms usually put an account manager between the client and the engineer. We don't. You get one senior practitioner who scopes the work, builds it, and tells you when an approach carries a risk your team will need to plan for. That last part is the reason engagements extend.

Being small is a constraint we work with rather than around. We take engagements where depth matters more than headcount, and we say no to the ones better served by a larger bench.

You don't hire us for reports. You hire us to get things done.
