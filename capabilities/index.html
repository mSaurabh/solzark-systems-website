Data engineering and architecture

When analysts rebuild the same extract by hand every month, the problem is rarely the report. We design analytical data stores, migrate legacy operational stores, and put an abstraction layer between reporting and the source schema so a schema change stops breaking dashboards.

What you get

  • A data model documented against the reporting the business actually runs
  • Pipelines with monitoring and a runbook your team can operate
  • A migration plan with dates, sequenced so operations keep running

Where it starts

A two-week assessment of the current stores, the reports depending on them, and the changes that keep breaking. You get the findings whether or not you continue.

02
RBACRow-level securityAD integration

Security and access control

Access models fail in one of two directions: too open to pass an audit, or so restrictive that people work around them. We design RBAC schemas and row-level security against the Active Directory groups you already maintain, and we apply confidentiality, integrity and availability as design constraints rather than a checklist at the end.

What you get

  • A documented role and permission model mapped to existing directory groups
  • Row-level security and schema-per-group tenancy where the data requires it
  • Evidence an auditor can read, produced during the work rather than after
Known risk

Tightening access surfaces the accounts that were quietly over-privileged. Expect a short window of access requests after cutover, and plan someone to own them.

QA and test automation

A suite nobody trusts costs more than no suite at all, because someone still spends their week triaging false failures. We build end-to-end frameworks on SerenityJS using the screenplay pattern, wired into Jenkins for nightly runs with alerting and reporting a product owner can act on.

What you get

  • A framework your engineers extend without calling us
  • Nightly execution in CI with automated failure alerting
  • Dashboards that report coverage in terms the business uses

Where it starts

A proof of concept on your highest-risk flow, reviewable before you commit to the full framework. One recent build reached 400+ automated tests in four months.

Cloud and AI integration

We build AWS-native services on Lambda, Bedrock and OpenSearch Serverless, defined in CloudFormation and released through the same pipeline as the rest of your platform. AI features are scoped against a measurable retrieval or accuracy target, not a demo. AWS AI Practitioner certified.

What you get

  • Infrastructure as code, so the environment is reproducible
  • Search or inference wired into your existing CI/CD
  • Cost and latency figures measured before rollout, not estimated
Known risk

Managed AI services move fast, and a model or endpoint you depend on can change under you. We pin versions and name the review interval in the handoff.
